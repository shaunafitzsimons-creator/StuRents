# UK & Ireland Student Lettings / HMO Proptech: Structural Shifts 2025–2026

Research conducted June 2026. All claims sourced inline.

---

## 1. Technology

**AI communication is hitting meaningful penetration in PBSA, not yet in HMO.**
VerbaFlo — an AI tenant communication tool — claims ~25% of UK PBSA beds (~168,000) by early 2026, and was tracking toward 40% by May 2026. Monthly revenue $100k; $1.6m contracted. Separately, industry analysis puts AI chatbots handling 78% of the 11 hours/week UK letting agents spend on repetitive tenant enquiries.
*Sources: PropertyWire 2025/26; Latch.co.uk 2026*

**Reapit shipped agentic lettings features in 2025.** Its RAI platform has live: AI-generated property descriptions, predictive lead scoring, adaptive scheduling, automated applicant screening, and a tenant-facing AI chatbot (Fi). These are in-market.
*Source: Latch.co.uk 2026*

**Portals are building into the tenancy workflow layer.** Rightmove launched "Lead to Keys" — an integrated suite covering tenancy progression, tenant referencing, and a renter checklist tracking application progress in real time — live October 2025. Zoopla added five integrated proptech partners (Goodlord, Vouch, SmartSearch) into its portal stack. This is a portal moving into territory previously owned by PMS and referencing vendors.
*Sources: Rightmove Press Centre Oct 2025; The Negotiator Oct 2025; Online Marketplaces*

**An EICR renewal wave is running now.** The 2020 mandatory EICR requirement for all tenancies hits its five-year renewal cycle in 2025–2026, creating spike demand for compliance-tracking tools like Fixflo.
*Source: Fixflo Lettings Software Buyers' Guide 2025*

## 2. Regulation

**The Renters' Rights Act received Royal Assent 28 October 2025; core provisions in force 1 May 2026.** Fixed-term ASTs are abolished. All assured tenancies become periodic. This breaks the traditional student lettings annual cycle.

**A new possession ground (4A) protects HMO student lets — but not small student lets.** Ground 4A allows landlords to recover possession at academic year-end where: the property is an HMO with 3+ unrelated tenants; tenants are full-time students at commencement; written notice of Ground 4A intent was given before the tenancy started; and four months' notice is served between June–September. One-to-two-bed student flats get no equivalent protection. This is the structural fault line in the new regime.
*Sources: Trowers & Hamlins Feb 2026; HEPI Nov 2025; Propertymark; Mishcon de Reya 2025*

**PBSA is exempt from the periodic tenancy regime.** Purpose-built student accommodation under Unipol or ANUK codes retains fixed-term common-law tenancies aligned to academic years.
*Source: Ashurst 2025*

**Rent in advance capped at one month.** This directly hits international students without UK guarantors, previously accommodated by multi-month upfront payments. Propertymark flagged this as unresolved as of August 2025.
*Source: Propertymark Aug 2025*

**HMO licensing is widening locally, steadily.** Nationally the framework is unchanged (5+ occupants), but 70+ councils now run additional licensing covering 3–4-occupant HMOs; 60+ English councils operate selective licensing. Leeds renewed and expanded from February 2026. Maximum civil penalties rising from £30,000 to £40,000 under the new Act.
*Sources: AgentHMO 2026; LetsafeUK 2026; Landlord Today Nov 2025*

**EPC C by 2030 is confirmed.** Landlord cost cap set at £10,000. EPC methodology overhaul due in 2026 — new system compulsory from October 2029, measuring heat retention rather than energy use. NRLA found 74% of student landlords with sub-EPC-C properties are considering selling — the highest proportion across any landlord segment.
*Sources: GOV.UK June 2026; Goodlord; NRLA*

**Ireland: nationwide rent controls effective 1 March 2026.** Student-specific accommodation (SSA) is included. Rent increases capped at 2% during high-inflation periods; new-build exempted. Ireland is running a structural bed deficit of ~15,000 student beds; a National Student Accommodation Strategy 2026–2035 was published.
*Sources: Irish Times Dec 2025; Gov.ie*

## 3. Economics

**PBSA occupancy fell sharply: 85.4% in 2025/26**, down 5.4 percentage points year-on-year. Pre-pandemic norms were 95–98%. CBRE described this as "a fairly dramatic decline in investor returns." Incentives (cashback, discounts) rose 15% annually and now equate to ~4.2% of advertised annual rent — operators trading margin for occupancy.
*Sources: Knight Frank Q4 2025 PBSA Report; HEPI Apr 2026; StuRents Feb 2025*

**PBSA investment remains strong at headline level.** £4.3bn committed in 2025, up 10% YoY; 79 deals. But occupancy softness is concentrating into specific markets — Nottingham lost over 4,000 first-year beds from its 2022/23 peak despite 7,700+ new PBSA beds opening.

**Rental growth decelerated hard: ~2% in 2025** versus 7.5% annually 2021–2024.
*Source: Knight Frank Q4 2025*

**International student demand is the leading indicator to watch.**
- HESA 2023/24 confirmed data (StuRents analysis): total international students fell 3.6% YoY; undergraduates -2.1%; postgraduates -4.5%
- European (excl. UK) students down 17.9% — sustained post-Brexit structural decline
- "Rest of world" posted its first decline in nearly a decade at -1.3%
- India (36%) has overtaken China (33%) at undergrad level — but Indian students are now also declining YoY
- Nigeria dropped 23.8% at postgraduate level YoY; Pakistan is a counter-trend at +39.4% postgraduate
- Coventry down 24.3% YoY — a city with 3,600 PBSA beds under construction (localised oversupply risk)
- Forward-looking visa data (GOV.UK): Q4 2025 applications -14.5% YoY; January 2026 applications -31% YoY
- 42 of 69 universities reported postgraduate enrolment declines for 2025/26
*Sources: StuRents/HESA May 2025; GOV.UK immigration stats Dec 2025; ICEF Monitor Jan 2026; HEPI*

**Private HMO landlords are exiting at scale.** ~93,000 landlords left the PRS in 2025; ~110,000 forecast for 2026. 15% of homes currently for sale were previously rentals. 74% of student landlords with below-EPC-C properties are considering selling. HMO yields remain at 7–9% for 2026, but compliance costs are compressing net returns for smaller operators.
*Sources: Pepper Money/Homenicom 2025; NRLA; HMO Sales 2026*

**Student affordability is structurally constrained.** Maximum maintenance loans are worth £1,200 less in real terms than 2021/22. HEPI estimates students need ~£20,000/year for normal participation; maximum loans provide ~£10,500. This is a ceiling on rental growth and is driving the incentive/discount escalation visible in PBSA.
*Source: HEPI Apr 2026*

**BTR encroachment into student demand.** BTR accounts for ~110,000 units nationally, with students comprising approximately 30% of occupancy and up to 40% in cities like Leeds.
*Source: Knight Frank Q4 2025*

## 4. Adjacent Categories Moving In

**Portals acquiring referencing territory.** Rightmove now offers tenant referencing as a service within its portal — a portal as a referencing vendor is a category boundary crossing. Zoopla embedded Goodlord and Vouch into its stack.
*Sources: Rightmove Press Centre; Letting Agent Today Jan 2025*

**Utilities and move-in services bundling into PMS onboarding.** Arthur Online, Veyco, and Just Move In formed a three-way partnership in March 2026 creating joined-up identity verification → tenancy → utilities activation at move-in. Canopy integrated Helpthemove's utility management directly into its platform for automated Council Tax, energy, and water notifications at move-in/out.
*Source: Letting Agent Today Mar 2026*

**Deposit alternatives widening adoption.** Flatfair, Reposit, and Zero Deposit are all operational and FCA-authorised. Reposit offers 8 weeks' landlord coverage; Flatfair charges tenants 28% of one month's rent. Being positioned as void-reduction tools given affordability constraints.
*Sources: Goodlord; Reposit; Letting Agent Today Apr 2025*

**A standalone compliance software layer is forming below horizontal PMS.** HMO Comply, August, and Managelet are narrow compliance trackers covering HMO licensing, EICR, gas safety, and EPC with council-ready exports. Expanding licensing schemes and rising civil penalties (now £40,000) are the demand driver.
*Sources: August 2025; HMO Comply*
